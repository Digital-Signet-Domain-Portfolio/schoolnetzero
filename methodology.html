Methodology: assumption register

Author: Oliver Wakefield-Smith, Founder Digital Signet. Reviewed against DfE Sustainability Strategy (Mar 2025) and Education Estates Strategy (Feb 2026). Verified 22 June 2026.

The methodology behind schoolnetzero.co.uk is published as an assumption register, modelled on a Salix bid appendix rather than as a prose narrative. Each row (A1 to A24) carries the assumption, the value, the primary source URL and the last-verified date. Auditors can cross-check the worksheet output line by line.

Grant offset application

The grant offset rule depends on the selected grant stack: PSDS only applies the PSDS Phase 4 cap (A9); PSDS plus Salix sums the PSDS cap (A9) and the CIF cap (A10) per school per year; Blended PPA applies the PSDS cap plus the solar element multiplied by the PPA fraction (A11). The output is always net of grant and ex-VAT.

Regional multiplier source

The regional multiplier (A3) draws from the 2025 Barbour ABI regional construction cost indices, which is the benchmark commonly applied in Salix PSDS bids and DfE Construction Framework call-offs. The multiplier ranges from 0.94 in Northern Ireland to 1.18 in London.
