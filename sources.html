Primary URLs back every claim

The /sources page lists the 27 primary URLs that back every numeric claim made across the site. Each citation [S1] to [S27] in body text resolves to a row in this register.

Last-verified date per source

All sources carry a last-verified date. The register is reviewed quarterly; specific sources (Ofgem cap, PSDS bid window) are checked at higher frequency.

Append-only vs versioned

Legislation (Climate Change Act 2022, DPDI Act) is append-only. Tariff caps (Ofgem) are versioned per quarter. PSDS phase guidance is versioned per cycle.

Auditor cross-check

A procurement officer auditing the worksheet inputs can follow the [S] number from any page to this register, then to the primary URL. The methodology page maps every assumption (A1 to A24) to one or more of these sources.
